Raunak Bhagate

I study applied AI and build ML systems that hold up once they're real. Most of that work is about efficiency. A system that is only good when it is large is not finished. I care about what it costs to run, what it can drop, and whether it stays useful once the budget is real.

The same standard shows up when a model has to act, not just answer. An agent is only as good as the limits around it: what it can see, what it is allowed to do, and how you tell that it did the right thing. And in geospatial work, the ground truth is often scarce, so a convincing map is not the same as a true one.

I am not collecting demos. I want systems you can trust after they leave the notebook.
